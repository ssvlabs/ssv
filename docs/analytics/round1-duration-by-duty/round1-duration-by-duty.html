Round‑1 QBFT takes the same time for every duty — except one

Once a leader proposes, how long does round‑1 consensus take to decide? Across proposer, aggregator, sync‑committee‑contribution and attester duties the median is ~50ms — the same intrinsic ~2‑hop latency. Only the attester is fat‑tailed. And the proposer's heavier tail turns out to be pure intra‑cluster drift: strip it out and it lands on the aggregator/sync band too.

Mechanism

Why three of four are tight — and the attester isn't

Same QBFT, same operators, same network — so the intrinsic latency is shared. What differs is what a non‑leader has to do to prepare, and that splits the attester off from the rest.

Proposer, aggregator, sync — settled data, structural check

A non‑leader validates a self‑contained value (a block, or an aggregate of messages that arrived earlier in the slot) with a structural‑only check, and accepts it the moment it arrives. Nothing waits on its own node re‑deriving the value. Tight, low‑variance timing.

Attester (committee) — arriving head, own‑vote check

The committee runner validates the proposed vote against its own node‑derived head for the current, still‑propagating block. An operator can only participate once its beacon node has settled that head — node‑to‑node import timing spreads the quorum out. Fat tail.

A note on when they decide

Tight duration is separate from when consensus starts. Aggregator and sync‑contribution decide ~8s into the slot (they wait two‑thirds of the slot for messages to aggregate) — far later than the proposer's ~1.4s or the attester's ~2.4s. But once they propose, round‑1 finishes just as fast. Round changes track the same split: aggregator 0.18%, sync 0.82%, proposer 0.17% — all far below the attester's 1.09%.

Isolating the drift

The proposer's extra tail is drift — and it separates cleanly

The proposer's tail lives in the prepare phase: in its slowest round‑1s the prepare spread is ~1,145ms while the commit spread is ~18ms. The quorum waits on its marginal operator, then commits fire fast. That wait carries two forms of drift — both measured from the leader's proposal.

Fast leader, slow pack — the dominant tail driver

In the extreme tail the leader got its block early and proposed while the rest of the cluster was still block‑fetching. A proposer non‑leader can only prepare once its own fetch completes — even though it doesn't need that block to validate the proposal.

Cap the wait → the proposer collapses onto the band

Capping the whole prepare‑quorum wait (proposal → 2f+1‑th prepare) at its typical drift‑free value (Q ≈ 19ms) removes both drift components: drift-removed = observed − max(wait − Q, 0). Well‑synced duties are untouched; drift duties shed only the excess wait. The proposer drops to p99 92ms, p99.9 212ms (dashed curve) — at or below aggregator/sync, which still carry a little drift of their own. All four converge on the intrinsic ~2‑hop QBFT latency.

Reading the tail honestly

  • The drift‑removed proposer's extreme max (1,095ms) is sample size, not a real cost. At fixed percentiles it is already tighter than aggregator/sync (p99.9 212ms vs 335/344ms). The longer visible max is only because the proposer has ~9× more duties (24,984 vs 2,746 / 6,897), so on a log‑survival plot its curve resolves farther down before its last sample. Subsampled to the aggregator's n, its max is ~493ms median [239–1,053] — below the aggregator's 626ms.
  • The 13 duties past 300ms are Exporter receive‑time noise, not latency. Each shows the prepare quorum forming instantly yet the decided stamped 0.3–1.1s later with commits bunched at a single instant (spread ≈ 0) — the signature of a late‑received decided message, sporadic across 13 different epochs.
  • Method. Duration is the Exporter's receive‑time of the round‑1 proposal and the aggregated decided message; the decided is gated by the marginal (2f+1‑th) operator. Aggregator is sampled across the validator population; sync‑contribution is targeted at each epoch's sync‑committee validators. Drift removal uses per‑operator prepare times from the raw proposer traces.
